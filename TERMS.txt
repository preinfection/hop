=========================================================================
PART 1: PRIVACY, IN SHORT
=========================================================================

Hop has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no tracking.
The apps do not send us your data, and we do not sell or share any.
Everything Hop reads stays on your PC, except what a feature you turned
on has to send to the service it uses (listed in the full privacy policy).

LOCATION. Hop does not use GPS, the Windows Location service, or your IP
address to find where you are. Weather, rain alerts and prayer times use
only the city you type in yourself.

Windows 11 may show "Location in use by: Network Command Shell" (netsh)
or by Hop Island. Windows treats the NAME of your Wi-Fi network as
location data, and Hop Island reads that name so it can show a pop-up
when your Wi-Fi connects or drops. The name never leaves your PC.
Turn off the Wi-Fi pop-up in Hop Island's settings and Hop stops reading it.

MICROPHONE AND CAMERA. Hop Island never records your microphone or camera.
It only checks WHICH apps are using them, to show the privacy dot. Hop
Clipper records your screen and desktop sound into memory so F8 can save
the last moments. It records your microphone only if you turn on the
microphone option. Nothing is saved until you press F8, clips are saved
only to the folder you chose, and they are never uploaded unless you
share one yourself.

OTHER THINGS HOP READS ON YOUR PC, AND NEVER SENDS ANYWHERE: your Windows
notifications (to show them as pop-ups), the name of the app in front,
the song playing, new files in Downloads and Screenshots, text you
copy (kept in memory only for clipboard history, gone when Hop closes),
and which Claude Code windows are open (from Claude Code's own files in
your user folder), so the AI agents page lists only the open ones.
